Great Arabic teaching requires more than language fluency. It takes a clear teaching approach that makes learning understandable, practical and engaging.

AFPAP builds on Dr. Saoussen Sodfi Mekni's experience in training teachers, writing textbooks and developing resources for non-native Arabic speakers, alongside the expertise of Formation Avenir in Paris.

Her approach draws on contemporary teaching practices in Europe, combining active learning, creative activities, the arts and play. She has supported thousands of teachers in making Arabic classes engaging, progressive and effective.

A specialist programme combining modern educational principles with practical ways to teach Arabic to non-native speakers. Each participant has opportunities to apply new skills.

01
Methods for teaching Arabic

Structure lessons, sequence skills and introduce concepts progressively.

02
Pedagogy and educational psychology

Understand learners' needs, individual differences and motivation.

03
Language skills and explanation techniques

Use language knowledge and translation when helpful to make content accessible.

04
Activities and teaching tools

Encourage participation through games, resources and purposeful activities.

05
Teaching practice: two micro-lessons

Each participant leads two short practice lessons to build classroom confidence and teaching skills.
